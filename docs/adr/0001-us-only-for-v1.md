# US only for V1, Canada deferred

Pill-Facts was conceived as a Canada-and-US product, and the repo still carries that
ambition, but the Health Canada Drug Product Database publishes no side-effect data of
any kind — only brand names, DINs, ingredients, strengths, forms and companies. The
only Canadian source for adverse reactions is the Product Monograph, which is available
solely as a PDF. Rather than ship a Canadian experience that is a DIN lookup with a
link, or present US labelling as though Health Canada had approved it, V1 is US only.

Canada returns when the monograph PDFs can be parsed into structured Safety Sections
with Provenance intact. Until then there is no Canadian Drug Concept.
